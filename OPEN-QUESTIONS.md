Lighthouse is a **witnessed knowledge commons carried by civic hubs** — a full JFA instance instantiating Part VI, with its own seven-point standard in `lighthouse-seed.md`.

## 1. The record-examination interface is undesigned
**Problem.** How members examine the witnessed record through their hub's app is unspecified.
**Constraints inherited.** Inherits the legibility ladder whole — decisions explaining themselves at point of use, rules as diffable policy data, policy versions witnessed, machine reading aids that explain but never certify (LEG-1, LEG-2). Named here rather than routed around.
**Status.** Open — the next design conversation.

## 2. LBTAS awaits its definition
**Problem.** The covenant's harm-surfacing assessment scale is treated as Leveson-derived, but its expansion and any Lighthouse-specific adaptation of the levels are unwritten.
**Constraints inherited.** Level semantics may narrow in gloss but never shift, or covenant comparability breaks across the federation (COV-1, COV-2); categories may adapt freely. Shared with the sibling LBTAS-based seeds.
**Status.** Open.

## 3. Geographic exclusion
**Problem.** A community with no participating hub cannot reach T4 (attested) and reaches T3 (cross-hub) only through distant registration.
**Constraints inherited.** A fix must not concentrate hub-provision; candidate design-arounds are traveling attestation days and partner-hub arrangements. This is the local face of architecture problem 8 (the witness cost floor excludes). Silent acceptance is refused.
**Status.** Open.

## 4. Hub capture beyond the scan
**Problem.** Real institutions can collude in ways the too-clean scan won't catch.
**Constraints inherited.** The answer is adjudication plus the operator-conduct read, both of which depend on federation being real — this problem rests on Stage 0 exactly as architecture problem 4 rests on problem 2. A fix must not install a central authority over hubs.
**Status.** Open — depends on the federated record (Stage 0).

## 5. The witness cost floor (architecture problem 8), and the JFA Part VII stack
**Problem.** Everything in the architecture's Part VII binds here — problems 2, 4, 7, 8 especially. Lighthouse's hub-as-witness design is a *proposed lever* on problem 8: civic institutions as long-lived, publicly accountable, mutually independent witnesses.
**Constraints inherited.** The lever must be proven in deployment, not assumed; a fix must not concentrate witness-provision in a few large hubs — the county-library-is-one-unit rule is the structural defense. Feed observations upward to Part VII.
**Status.** Open — Lighthouse is a candidate contribution to problem 8, pending real federation.

## 6. Sequencing note (from Part VIII development order)
**Problem.** Not a problem but a committed order: Stage 0 (federated two-hub record) is the prerequisite for every tier above T1 reading honestly, and the economy inflow switch (Stage 4) is gated behind the regulatory read and governed policy.
**Constraints inherited.** Build bottom-up; each stage ships its stand-in labels honest (the launch floor is two independent hubs; a single-hub pilot carries the stand-in label). Weight sizes nothing until the governed, witnessed switch — Sybil-before-citation-weight satisfied by ordering.
**Status.** Committed sequence; Stage 0 is the highest-leverage build (architecture problem 2 in Lighthouse's clothes).
